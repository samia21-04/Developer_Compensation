# Jamal Part B distribution findings

The prepared sample contains 4,435 U.S. employed respondents. Median annual total compensation is $150,000, compared with mean $168,300. The middle half runs from $110,000 to $200,000 (IQR $90,000). Compensation remains right-skewed after the preparation cutoffs; the raw and natural-log histograms describe the same retained respondents.

Median reported professional work experience is 14 years. Experience is missing for 35 respondents; these blanks may represent zero years or nonresponse. They remain visible in categorical counts and are excluded from numerical experience comparisons.

The nine prepared role families have at least 265 respondents each. Work-arrangement plots show both the three prepared groups and the original five choices; the prepared Hybrid group includes Your choice. Missing work arrangement is recorded for 17 respondents and missing education for 5.

These summaries describe the prepared sample, after excluding low and high pay in notebook 01. They do not describe the untrimmed distribution or establish full-time status. All source rows in the processed CSV are retained by notebooks 02 and 03.
